Introduction:
Overview of the M-Integrative Solver and its applications in human trafficking investigations.

Ethical Guidelines:
- Adhere to data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).
- Ensure fairness and non-discrimination in predictive modeling.
- Maintain transparency and accountability in decision-making.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
Accuracy: Percentage of correct predictions compared to real-world outcomes
Fairness: Absence of disproportionate targeting of specific groups or regions
Transparency: Availability of algorithms and data sources for review
Accountability: Clear lines of responsibility for decisions based on Solver outputs
Privacy Compliance: Adherence to data protection laws and anonymization practices

Evaluation Metrics:
Accuracy: Compare predicted risk scores to actual trafficking incidents
Fairness: Analyze flagged hubs for demographic or regional bias
Transparency: Audit the Solver’s algorithms and data sources
Accountability: Review decision-making processes and outcomes
Privacy Compliance: Conduct regular data audits and ensure anonymization

Case Studies:
Real-world scenarios demonstrating the responsible use of the Solver.
